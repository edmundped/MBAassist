# MBAassist UI audit: Managerial Economics warm-up and shared shell

Auditor role: senior UI designer. Date: Fri 27 Nov 2026 (MBA645 day 1).
Method: Playwright and Chrome at 390x844, 360x740 and 1280x860, light and dark (`mba512-theme` and `color_scheme`). Touch was emulated with CDP touch events. Contrast was measured in the page with a script that resolves backgrounds and blends alpha, and key token pairs were computed by hand (WCAG 2.x). Screenshots are in `/home/claude/audit/shots/ui-*.png`. Line numbers refer to `/home/claude/MBAassist/index.html` as it is on disk now. No project files were changed.

## 1. Verdict

1. The base "Harmattan Ledger" system is good. Body text contrast passes in both themes (ink-3 is 5.4:1 light and 6.0:1 dark), and the type and colour tokens are disciplined. The problems come from **three CSS layers on top of each other**: the base, "Friendly redesign" (l.1363) and "Resources editorial" (l.1437). Each restyles the last, so radii, shadows, buttons and even whole components (`.plan`, `.statrow`) contradict each other.
2. The explorers are the best idea in the app, but right now they **break when touched**. Dragging the price slider on a deck card swipes you to another card. The demand explorer draws its point *off* the shifted curve. The colour coding shows "inelastic" as a red error state.
3. The fun features (visual cards, explorers, XP) are **hidden**. The deck is reached by a small underlined text link inside a lesson or by the first card on Practise. The Plan tab, where students land, shows neither the cards nor XP, and the hub lists the course that starts today last.
4. Dark mode is mostly solid, with one blocker: the deck's main **Next button is white on light indigo at 2.25:1**. Card edges disappear in dark mode (border 1.11:1, and the shadows cannot be seen on a near-black background).
5. Overall the warm-up feels **dense and academic, not playful**. The same 14-row "To do" list appears four times, launch day reads "0 days until MBA645 begins", and the deck stacks two headers. About ten targeted CSS/markup fixes would turn it into the friendly tool the students asked for.

## 3. Make it delightful (cheap, on-identity)

1. **"Today's card" hero on the hub**: a single indigo card that says "MBA645 starts today · 0/14 ready", with a mini-deck preview (three fanned `.pdcard` outlines at ±4° rotation) and one button: "Play 4 minutes".
2. **Draggable dot with a halo**: make the explorer dot the control. A soft pulsing ring (`@keyframes` on `r` and opacity) shows until the first drag, and a small "+5 XP · You found the revenue peak!" toast appears when the student lands on P=50 or Q=40.
3. **Revenue rectangle that celebrates**: in elast, tint the TR rectangle `--ochre` and briefly flash it (`fill-opacity` .16 → .32) when revenue hits its maximum. Counting the readout numbers with `requestAnimationFrame` makes the change feel physical.
4. **Kente-stripe progress**: replace the grey `.pdots` with a woven stripe. Each completed card adds a coloured segment that cycles through the existing role hues (indigo, teal, ochre, laterite, plum), which ties the progress bar to the Ghanaian setting at no asset cost.
5. **Flip cards for "The idea"**: turn "Reveal the idea ›" into a real 3D flip (`transform:rotateY(180deg)`, `backface-visibility:hidden`) of the whole card, with "Tap to flip" in the empty space that today shows almost nothing.
6. **Streak flame on the Plan and in the deck bar**: reuse `.dash-streak` from Finance (gold pill, "Day 1 🔥"-style glyph as SVG) next to XP so the warm-up has the same reward rhythm as Finance.
7. **Topic constellation on Progress**: show the 14 topics as dots in four unit clusters, which fill with indigo as they become ready. Built from the existing `.ptdot` state classes, it makes a calm "map of what you know".
8. **Haptic and sound-free feedback**: `navigator.vibrate?.(12)` on a correct answer and when the dot crosses MR=MC, plus a 120ms scale-pop on the XP chip (`.pxp` keyframe) whenever XP changes.
