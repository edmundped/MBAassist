Quiz guide

  • Classify decisions first: capital budgeting invests in real assets; financing raises money through debt or equity; payout returns cash to owners.
  • For Lecture 1, master shareholders, board oversight, agency problems, real vs financial assets, primary vs secondary markets, and intermediaries.
  • For Lecture 2, master ratio purpose, MVA/EVA, ROC/ROA/ROE, efficiency ratios, leverage, liquidity, DuPont, and sustainable growth.
  • For TVM sample questions, identify whether the cash flow is a lump sum, annuity, annuity due, perpetuity, or delayed/growing stream before calculating.
